What the app does today

Version 0.1 does not keep the route. While a trip runs, the phone reads GPS every 2 to 4 seconds, adds up the distance between points, and throws the points away. Each saved trip has only its date, miles and odometer. So the map needs a small change to keep those points, and trips recorded before that change will not have a map.

What you would get

  • A route preview on every trip in the list, and a full map when you tap it.
  • Start and end pins named after your saved places (Home, Work) or the gas station, matching the gas station proposal.
  • Start and end times, minutes driven, average and top speed, odometer before and after.
  • "Log fill-up here" on trips that end at a gas station.
  • Delete one route (the miles stay), or all routes, from settings.

Storage

The app keeps a point only when the car has moved, then trims straight stretches when the trip ends, so a typical mile needs about 10 to 20 points. That works out to roughly 200 bytes per mile, or about 3 MB for 15,000 miles a year. Routes go in their own file, separate from your logbook, so the main data stays small and fast. Android's backup allows 25 MB for the app, which leaves room for several years.

Map images you look at are cached separately (typically 10 to 30 MB, capped, and you can clear them).

Battery and data

No extra battery while driving. GPS is already running for every trip to measure the miles; keeping the points just saves what is already being read. Nothing runs between trips.

Opening a map downloads the map images for that area, a few hundred KB the first time and nothing after that for places you have already looked at. Downloads happen only when you open a map.

Privacy

Routes stay on the phone. When you open a map, the map server sees which area's images are requested, the same as any map app, but it never gets your route or your trips.

Effort

About two days of app work: keep and trim the points, the trip map screen, the list previews and the settings. It shares the trip end point with the gas station feature, so building both together saves a little time.
