You are helping me build an Android app called Garage Log (working name). It replaces MotoLog and tracks mileage, fuel, service records and reminders, costs, and CSV import/export across multiple vehicles.

How the app works: each vehicle is linked to a Bluetooth device (its car radio or hands-free). When my phone connects, a trip starts and phone GPS measures the miles. When it disconnects, the trip ends and the miles are added to that vehicle's odometer. Whichever vehicle is connected becomes the active profile, and all new entries (fuel, service, expenses) default to it, with a manual override.
