Garage Log simulator

A working prototype with sample data. Use the panel to get in a vehicle: connecting its Bluetooth starts a trip, and disconnecting adds the miles to that vehicle. Everything in the phone is tappable. Your changes stay in this browser until you reset.

Car Bluetooth

Devices paired with the phone. Connect one as if you just started the car. Only linked devices start a trip.

Where you stop

Where the car is when you shut it off (Bluetooth disconnects) or open Log fill-up mid-trip. The real app reads this from GPS.
